# Privacy Policy - NetPulse

Last updated: 26 October 2026

**NetPulse does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data. The optional Speed Test exchanges anonymous test data with a public speed-test server; see below.**

## What the app reads (on your device only)
- Total network traffic counters from Android (`TrafficStats`) to show live speed.
- Android's own data-usage statistics (`NetworkStatsManager`) to show daily, monthly and per-app usage. This needs the "Usage access" permission you grant.
- Installed-app names and icons, only to label the per-app usage list.

## What the app stores
Only your settings (theme, size, position, data-plan limit, billing day) in the app's private storage on your phone. The app keeps no usage log or history of its own.

## What the app never does
- It uses the `INTERNET` permission only for the Speed Test you start yourself. That test sends and receives anonymous dummy data with Cloudflare's public speed-test servers (no personal data, no identifiers added by the app). Nothing else in the app touches the internet.
- No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, no third-party SDKs.
- It does not read messages, contacts, files, photos, location or the content of any other app.

## Your control
Revoke any permission in Android settings, or uninstall the app, and all of its data is removed.
